Pool settings

A pause retains reservations and observes confirmations. Transactions already signed may still confirm. Resuming co-mining permits only the configured operator-funded stake and near-miss operations.

Fees apply only to newly assigned work. Existing shares keep their fee, and existing payment batches keep their minimum. Receipts always need at least 120 confirmations and actual spendability. Staking yield is shared; operator principal and fee funds stay separate.

Reconciliation

Recheck canonical income and existing signed payments. This cannot create a payment, sign a transaction, clear a deficit, reset records or release reserved inputs.
