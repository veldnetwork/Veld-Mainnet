From node to pool in four steps

YOUR KEYS STAY WITH YOU
  1. 01

    Open Veld Node

    Install Veld Node from the official Veld website and choose the Pool tab. Solo mining remains on the Mining tab.

  2. 02

    Choose your pool and payout address

    Use https://pool.veld.network and enter your own Veld payout address. Leave the optional certificate field blank for Veld Pool. You can also choose another HTTPS pool endpoint.

    Pool endpoint: —

  3. 03

    Choose CPU workers and start

    Select how many CPU workers to use, then Start pool mining. The node reports accepted work and reconnects after interruptions. No deposit, personal stake or wallet passphrase is needed.

  4. 04

    See your earnings

    Choose Copy view access in the node and paste it below. Earnings remain assigned to your account when you disconnect. Stop disables automatic pool resume.

Connection help

Check the endpoint and network first. Veld Pool uses your system’s trusted certificates. Other pools may supply a custom certificate authority; never disable certificate verification. “Busy; retrying” means the worker is waiting for resources; it does not mean your proof is invalid. Keep the private account folder when reinstalling. Changing the payout address creates a separate account and does not move earlier earnings.

How rewards and payments work

Block rewards

Your verified work earns a proportional share using a rolling PPLNS window of two network blocks of expected work. Only actual pool miner outputs create earnings.

Co-mining & staking

When funded and enabled, actual lottery winnings and staking yield are shared with contributors from the earning window. The operator’s contributed stake principal stays separate.
