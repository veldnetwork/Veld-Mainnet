GOOD TO KNOW

Before you start.

Simple answers for your first session.

Do I need VELD or a stake to join?

No. Pool miners need a payout address and computing power. No deposit or personal stake is required. The operator separately funds any pool stake and transaction fees.

Why are my rewards pending?

A reward needs at least 120 canonical confirmations and actual spendability. It then becomes available for a scheduled payout batch. Your payout address must reach the published minimum; smaller balances carry forward.

Can I mine from two computers?

Yes. Each client can have its own account and work record. Use the same personal payout address to qualify for the minimum together. Each account’s viewing access shows that account’s earnings.

Why has the network node count not increased?

A pool worker connects to the pool’s gateway. It is not a separate full node in the network peer count. The pool submits blocks through its full-node backend.

What does “busy; retrying” mean?

The client is waiting for connection or verification resources and retries automatically. A temporary resource limit does not make your proof invalid. Check the endpoint, your connection and the service health above if it persists. Never disable certificate verification.

What happens when I stop or reinstall?

Stopping disables automatic pool resume; it does not remove earnings. Preserve your client’s private account folder when reinstalling. Keep viewing access private. A new account or payout address does not transfer previous earnings.

When will the pool find its next block?

Block discovery is probabilistic. More verified work increases the pool’s expected contribution, but no countdown guarantees a block or daily income. The hashrate estimate uses recently verified shares and can fluctuate.
