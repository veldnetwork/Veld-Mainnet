How-to / Wallet

Send and receive VELD safely

Share the correct receive address, review every send before signing, and confirm the transaction without exposing your keyfile or private key.

Open Veld WalletAbout 7 minutes

01 / Receive

Share the address, never the key

A Veld address begins with V and is safe to share. A private key, keyfile, or passphrase is never required to receive funds.

  1. Open the wallet and select Receive

    Unlock the intended wallet, then use the Receive action on the Balance page.

  2. Copy the address or show its QR code

    Use the wallet’s copy control or let the sender scan the displayed QR code.

  3. Verify what the sender received

    Compare the beginning and end of the address through a second channel, especially for a first payment or large amount.

Clipboard malware can replace copied addresses. Always compare the address after pasting. For a new recipient, consider a small test payment first.

02 / Enter

Prepare the send

Open Send VELD and unlock the keystore so signing stays local. Avoid manually entering a raw private key unless recovery requires it.

From Address
The unlocked wallet that will spend the funds.
To Address
Paste, scan, or choose a previously verified address from Book.
Amount
Enter an exact amount or use the 25%, 50%, 75%, or Max helpers. Leave room for the displayed network fee.
Memo
Optional, limited to 80 bytes, and stored publicly on-chain. Never put secrets or personal data in it.

03 / Review

Check the transaction before signing

Select Review & send, then slow down. Support cannot cancel a network-accepted transfer. A recipient can send a separate refund, but that does not reverse the original transaction.

  • The complete recipient address matches the intended destination.
  • The amount and decimal placement are correct.
  • The fee estimate is acceptable.
  • The source wallet has enough spendable balance.
  • The memo contains nothing private.
If anything is unexpected, cancel. Clear the form and obtain the recipient address again rather than editing around a suspicious value.

04 / Confirm

Verify broadcast and confirmation

  1. Sign and broadcast

    Approve the reviewed transaction. The wallet signs locally and submits it to the network.

  2. Check wallet history

    Use the Sent/Fees or Received history view to see the transaction and its block status.

  3. Confirm independently when needed

    Open the public explorer and search the transaction or address. Do not share a private key to prove payment.

    https://explorer.veld.network
Pending is not the same as lost. Wait for network confirmation before retrying. A block confirmation can change during a chain reorganization; check that the transaction remains on the canonical chain. Re-sending can create a second payment if the first was already accepted.

Safety checklist

  • Use the official wallet hostname.
  • Confirm pasted addresses after every copy.
  • Use a small test amount for a new high-value destination.
  • Never place secrets in the public memo field.
  • Never share a keyfile, passphrase, or private key to receive support.
