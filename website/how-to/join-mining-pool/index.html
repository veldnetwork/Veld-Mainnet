How-to / Pool mining

Join Veld Pool

Use your own payout address, choose your CPU workers and mine together. Your wallet keys stay with you.

Veld Node 3.2.3 · About 5 minutes
01Connect 02Mine 03Rewards 04Your account

01 / Connect

Open the Pool tab

  1. Choose the pool endpoint

    In Veld Node, open Pool. Set Pool endpoint (HTTPS) to https://pool.veld.network. You can enter another compatible pool instead.

  2. Enter your payout address

    Use an address from your own Veld wallet. Use the same address on your PC and laptop if you want both machines paid to that wallet. Do not enter the pool operator’s wallet address.

  3. Select CPU workers

    Start with enough spare capacity for your other apps. Leave the optional certificate authority field blank for Veld Pool. Choose Start pool mining.

No wallet import or deposit. Joining does not require your keyfile, private key, wallet passphrase or a personal stake. Installing the client does not connect or start mining automatically.

02 / Mine

Check accepted work

The Pool tab shows connection state, measured hashrate and accepted work. Allow time for the dataset to initialize. A submitted share becomes credited work only after the pool verifies it.

Mining
The client is working on a current pool job. Watch accepted work advance.
Retrying
A connection or verifier is temporarily unavailable. The client retries automatically; a busy verifier does not make your proof invalid.
Stale work
The chain advanced before that job completed. It is different from an invalid proof or a duplicate submission.

Use Stop pool mining before changing the endpoint, payout address or CPU settings. Starting enables resume on this device; Stop turns resume off. To return to solo mining, stop pool mining and use the Mining tab.

More threads are not always faster. Compare steady hashrate after warm-up. Memory bandwidth, temperature and other programs can reduce performance at higher worker counts.

03 / Rewards

Understand your balance

Pending
Your allocated share of actual income, waiting for canonical confirmations and spendability.
Available
Mature, reconciled earnings that can enter a payment batch once the threshold and schedule are met.
Paid
A payment confirmed on the canonical chain. Follow its transaction ID and confirm it in your wallet.

Block rewards use verified work in the pool’s recent PPLNS window. Two machines can earn different amounts even when they use the same payout address. Work from earlier contributors can still fall inside that window; only the miner-category output is shared as block income.

When the pool identity qualifies for co-mining, actual lottery winnings and its ordinary staking yield are shared with the contributors in their recorded windows. Members do not need a personal stake. Operator-contributed stake principal remains separate.

The candidate settings are 0% service fee, 1 VELD payment threshold, daily batches and at least 120 confirmations plus actual spendability. Eligible accounts using the same payout address are combined for the destination threshold. Earnings below it remain recorded. Check the dashboard for the active terms; daily batches do not guarantee daily earnings.

04 / Your account

See rewards and payment history

Select Copy view access, then Open dashboard. Paste the viewing access into the account sign-in form. Public pool statistics do not require private account access.

Keep the viewing access private. It is separate from your payout wallet keys. Using the same payout address on two machines does not automatically reveal either machine’s private account details to the other.

Stopping, disconnecting or restarting does not erase earned balances. Preserve the client’s account records when moving installations. Changing a payout address starts a separate account profile; it does not transfer or rewrite earlier entitlements.
