How to stake VELD

From finding your keyfile and signing in to staking, tracking rewards, and withdrawing after your lockup. Start here, even if you have never opened Veld Wallet.

01 / Prepare

Find your keyfile and sign in

Find the file for your existing wallet

If you mine with Veld Node: open the desktop client, then Settings → Local paths → Open data folder. Look for veld-wallet-<address prefix>.veld-keys beside miner.key. Use the .veld-keys file.

If you created a browser wallet: check your device’s Downloads folder or browser download history for a file ending in .veld-keys. On iPhone, use Files → Browse and check Downloads under iCloud Drive or On My iPhone. On Android, check Files → Downloads.

To use a phone, transfer a copy to your own device with AirDrop, a cable, or another trusted device-to-device method, then save it in Files. Keep a separate encrypted backup and store its passphrase separately. Never share either with another person.

No wallet yet? Follow Create and back up a wallet, then return here. If you already mine, use that wallet’s file to access its funds.

Open and unlock Veld Wallet

  1. Open wallet.veld.network and check the exact HTTPS address.
  2. Select Log in. On mobile, you can also open More → Log in.
  3. Select Import keystore file and choose your .veld-keys file. If this browser already holds the intended wallet, use its Unlock screen instead.
  4. Enter the passphrase for that file and select Unlock. If importing an encrypted miner.key instead, use Decrypt & Import. Importing gives you access to the same wallet; it does not move your VELD.

Open the Wallet tab and compare the complete address with your mining client or known wallet address. If it differs, stop and choose the correct file. Once unlocked, Wallet Settings → Save file downloads another encrypted backup.

Check your funds before staking

  • Check your spendable balance. From block 3,840, each new ordinary stake needs at least 500 VELD plus a network fee. Below that height, the minimum is 1,000 VELD. Maturing rewards, locked funds, and pending spends are unavailable. Fund your wallet if needed.
  • Open the Stake tab. Confirm your address. One address can hold up to 10,000 VELD in active stakes.

02 / Lock

Choose your amount and term

Under Lock more VELD, select a Tier & lockup and enter your Amount.

Lockup options

Long43,200 blocksAbout 90 days1.50×

Days assume the 180-second target block interval. The unlock height determines when you can unstake. Weight adjusts your share of vault distributions; it is not an interest rate.

  • Select Review & lock. Check the address, amount, tier, lockup, and fee before approving.
  • Wait for confirmation. The wallet signs locally and broadcasts the transaction. Save the transaction ID; do not resubmit while it is pending.
  • Check your stake. Look for the confirmed amount in Staked and the tier and unlock height in staking history.
Choose a term you can leave locked. Staked funds cannot be sent, swapped, or consolidated. Reaching the unlock height lets you unstake; it does not withdraw the funds automatically.

03 / Rewards

Follow your vault rewards

Vault distributions run every 480 blocks. Follow the wallet’s next-distribution counter and confirmed activity. Your share depends on eligible stake weight, available vault funds, and the network’s distribution limits.

You can close the wallet between visits. Your ordinary stake remains on-chain and does not require the browser to stay open.

04 / Unstake

Withdraw when your stake matures

Return to Stake and check Unstakeable now. Enter no more than that mature amount in the unstake Amount field, then select Unstake.

Unstaking pays a network fee. After confirmation, check that your staked balance has decreased and the funds, less the fee, are spendable again. Ordinary wallet stakes have no further holding cooldown after a valid, confirmed unstake.

Each position has its own unlock height, so a newer stake may still be locked.

For miners

Join the co-mining lottery

Co-mining combines staking with qualifying mining work. Its minimum remains 1,000 VELD; the ordinary-staking reduction to 500 VELD does not change lottery eligibility. Staking alone does not enter you into a draw.

  • Use the same address. Keep at least 1,000 VELD staked under your miner’s address, plus a small spendable balance for share transactions and fees.
  • Keep mining. Update your client and follow Start CPU mining. Check that it is hashing on the validated chain.
  • Let the client submit work. It automatically submits qualifying near-miss shares. These must be accepted on-chain in the relevant window for eligibility.
  • Follow the draw. Windows are 100 blocks. Check the wallet’s next-draw counter and confirmed rewards. Pair with the Portal to monitor your miner remotely.

Mining to another address does not use this stake. Falling below the required staked amount removes that address’s eligibility.

Need a hand?

I cannot find my keyfile or unlock the wallet

Use Import a keyfile for file locations and recovery steps. If the node’s portable file is missing, sign in to your existing encrypted identity with the latest client and check its data folder again. Preserve miner.key; do not delete it or create a replacement identity. If the wallet is already unlocked in another browser, export it through Wallet Settings → Save file.

The browser opens a different wallet

Back up that wallet before signing out or replacing it. Use a separate browser profile to import your intended file, then compare the complete address. Keep the original wallet and its backup intact.

My balance is enough, but Available is too low

Only spendable funds cover a new stake and its fee. Wait for mining rewards to mature or pending transactions to confirm.

Review & lock is disabled or my transaction is pending

Unlock the wallet and let live staking status load. Check the transaction ID and wait for any pending stake to confirm before retrying.

My stake still shows as locked

Check its unlock height and the Unstakeable now amount. Calendar-day labels are estimates; block height controls maturity.

Do I need to register as a validator?

No. This guide covers ordinary wallet staking. Validator registration is a separate role with separate requirements in the mainnet rules.
