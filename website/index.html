Why Veld

Four design choices.
No compromise on any of them.

All four are implemented in the source; launch-gated features remain closed until their on-chain activation conditions are met.

Post-quantum native signatures

Native VELD transaction and finality signatures use ML-DSA-65. Native addresses currently use 160-bit HASH160 commitments, while btcVELD custody inherits Bitcoin Taproot’s secp256k1/Schnorr assumptions.

Memory-hard PoW

VeldHash is a deterministic, memory-hard CPU proof of work built around a 1 GB dataset. A laptop, desktop, or home rig can run the same full node and mining client.

Rewards follow participation

Before staking activates, ordinary blocks split 50% to the miner and 50% to the vault. After activation, ordinary blocks split 50% miner, 20% co-mining, 20% vault, and 10% validator pool. Combined multipliers cap at 3.00×.

Consensus-native. No VM.

Core features are enforced directly by every node instead of a general-purpose smart-contract VM. btcVELD is a separately gated, Bitcoin-backed consensus asset; its issuer cannot override custody, mint, redeem, or supply rules.

How it works

Six ways to participate.
One network, one wallet.

Mine, run a full node, stake, validate, co-mine, or provide liquidity when its activation gate opens.

— 01 MINE

Run the client

Download Veld Node and open Veld Node. The app validates the chain, manages your mining identity, and starts VeldHash mining when synchronization completes. The winning miner receives 50% of the block reward directly at their address.

Block reward: 3.1393 VELD · ~180s cadence
— 02 STAKE

Lock VELD, earn from vault

After canonical issued supply reaches 10,000 VELD mined, lock at least 1,000 VELD for 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days. Longer lockups earn higher multipliers (1.00× → 1.50×). Every 480 blocks, the vault distributes up to 90% of that cycle's inflow and retains the rest as principal.

Vault fills with: 50% of ordinary blocks before activation; 20% after · + 100% of every 100th block
— 03 VALIDATE

Endorse blocks, sign for rewards

Once staking is active, bond 10,000 VELD and register your ML-DSA-65 public key. Your daemon endorses new blocks, and the endorsement pool pays active validators every 480 blocks.

Endorsement pool: 10% of ordinary block rewards after staking activation
— 04 CO-MINE

Get paid for near-misses

Mining contributes even when a hash does not win the block. Qualifying near-misses can place active miners into the co-mining draw, where the pool is shared among selected participants. The client handles submission automatically—run it, stay connected, and let the network account for your work.

Co-mine pool: 20% of ordinary block rewards after staking activation
— 05 PROVIDE

Provide pool liquidity

After btcVELD completes its one-time seven-validator activation, pool VELD with 1:1 Bitcoin-backed btcVELD and earn the fee retained by each swap. The entire pool fee accrues to liquidity providers; there is no protocol cut, and liquidity removal remains available.

Swap fee: 0.30%–1.00% four-band · 100% to providers · exit always open
— 06 VERIFY

Run your own full node

Don't trust, verify. The same client that mines runs a full P2P node: it independently validates every block, native transaction and finality signatures, and every coinbase split. Nothing about the chain's state depends on a central server you have to trust.

RAM: ~1.2 GB (VeldHash dataset) · Disk: grows with chain history

Get started

Three steps to a running node.
Everything else is optional.

Download the client, pick a wallet, leave it running. No sign-up, no account, no KYC — your address is your identity.

— STEP 01

Download & extract

Grab VeldClient-Windows-x64.zip from below. Extract anywhere on your PC. No install or administrator access is required, and your node syncs from the network’s relay anchors on first run.

— STEP 02

Open Veld Node

Open Veld Node, then create a protected mining identity or import an encrypted .veld-keys wallet file from Settings.

— STEP 03

Leave it running

The node syncs with peers, then mining starts automatically. Mining auto-tunes across available physical cores while reserving roughly one core for the system. Close the window to stop. Everything you earn lands at your address directly.

— FIRST LAUNCH

What you'll see

The native app keeps first-time setup in one place:

01 Create a new encrypted mining identity
02 Import an encrypted wallet keyfile (.veld-keys)
03 Choose Clearnet or Tor-only networking
04 Start mining after chain synchronization

The selected identity becomes the mining reward address and automatically endorses blocks when that same address is registered as a validator.

— ADVANCED

Validator setup

Extra requirements if you want to endorse blocks for a share of the endorsement pool:

  • ▸Stake the validator minimum on a single address (10,000 VELD)
  • ▸Register pubkey via wallet's Validators tab
  • ▸Keep the node online 24/7 to endorse every block
  • ▸Payouts cadence: every 480 blocks (daily)

Download

One app. Node, miner, validator.

The desktop app runs a full P2P node, mines blocks, and endorses automatically when its identity is registered as a validator. Clearnet is the default, with Tor-only networking available in Settings.

Veld Node 3.0.4

Self-contained for Windows 10 / 11 with no bundled DLL files and no MSYS2 requirement. Extract it and double-click Start Veld Node.bat to start the signed client on clearnet. Windows may request approval once to allow inbound peer traffic on TCP 8333. RPC and local app ports remain closed. Initial sync uses the current official signed snapshot when eligible and otherwise safely validates from genesis; snapshot state is independently revalidated from genesis in the background. After sign-in the node keeps one portable encrypted .veld-keys file beside miner.key, and Settings can request a new portal pairing code.
